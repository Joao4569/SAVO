SAVO Satellite Meeting in Veterinary Ophthalmology – February 6, 2027

The Swiss Association of Veterinary Ophthalmologists (SAVO) is delighted to announce its first Satellite Meeting, taking place on February 6, 2027 as part of the KIS Congress.

This three-hour event will feature talks on new topics and exciting developments in veterinary ophthalmology. The meeting aims to foster exchange and discussion among those interested in ophthalmology, practising veterinarians and specialists in the field.

We particularly encourage participants to contribute their own short talks or case presentations, creating an interactive forum for sharing experiences, ideas and knowledge.

Whether you have extensive experience in ophthalmology or are simply enthusiastic about the field, we look forward to welcoming you to this new SAVO initiative!
